Decisions

  • The degradation order is fixed and the same everywhere: ornament → counts → secondary meta → structure → content. Nothing in the last two categories is ever dropped to make room for something in the first two.
  • Headers clip counts before titles. AGENTS · 9 becomes AGENTS, and the amber 2 need you survives — because a total is trivia, and the number of things blocking you is not. The group captions keep their counts, since that is where counting actually pays.
  • Cards collapse to one line; the selected card keeps its second. One line is enough to identify and choose; the selection is the only row you are currently reasoning about, so it keeps the question or the activity. Detail moves from a side panel to a full-screen push on ⏎.
  • Composers always survive intact — prompt char, draft, mode label, and at minimum ⏎/esc. A narrow terminal that can't show you what mode you're typing into is worse than one that shows less transcript.
  • Two-column layouts become one column, not two cramped ones. Below 110 columns the diff drops its file index and the agents view drops its inspector, rather than squeezing both into unreadable halves.
  • Commands wrap, never truncate. A verbatim command that has to be scrolled to be read would break the promise the approval card makes, so at 70 columns it wraps with a ↳ marker and the card grows instead.
  • The statusbar keeps cwd and the blocked indicator and gives up the branch dirt-count and version first. F-keys shrink to bare numbers — you already know what they do by the second day, and the amber (1 waiting) is the one that must never disappear.
