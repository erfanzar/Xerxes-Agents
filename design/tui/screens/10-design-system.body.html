XERXES TUI — DESIGN SYSTEM 150 × 40 cells · 13px / 18px · v0.4.1

1 · grid every measurement is a cell, never a pixel

1 cell = 1ch × 18px. Gutters are 2ch, narrow gutters 1ch.
Whitespace is blank rows, not fractional padding — so a mock and a render agree on line counts.
Chrome heights are fixed: header 2 rows, footer 2 rows, statusbar 1 row. 35 rows are left for content at 40 rows tall.

4 · state the same dot means the same thing on every screen

● blue#6ea8feworking / running · also the brand
● green#83c99ddone · succeeded · added lines
● red#dd7c88failed · nonzero exit · removed lines
● amber#d8ae58blocked · needs you · warnings ONLY
violet#b18be8agent talking about itself (activity)
cyan#56c2d6structure markers — hunk headers
Blue is both brand and "working". That is deliberate: the tool's own colour is the colour of it doing its job.
Amber never means "in progress". If it is amber, a human is the blocker.
Never a 7th colour for a 7th state — add a glyph or a caption instead.
Never amber for emphasis, headings, or the brand mark.

8 · rules non-negotiable

Every screen answers where am I (header), what's happening (body + state dots), what can I do (footer).
Overlays restore the previous screen on cancel, and name what you keep: esc cancel (stays on opus-5).
Deny is the Esc default on anything that asks permission.
Truncation is announced (▾ 2 more), never silent.
No key or button on screen that isn't wired to a real capability.
No badge for a fact the data doesn't have — a missing price shows nothing, not "n/a".
No colour used decoratively. If it isn't state, it's grey.
No spinner without an elapsed time next to it.

Decisions

  • Six state colours is the budget, and it is closed. Blue, green, red, amber, violet, cyan — each with a single sentence of meaning. New states get a glyph or a caption; a seventh hue would break the promise that colour is readable without a legend.
  • Five greys, each with one job. The ramp exists so that the eye is drawn by contrast rather than by colour: on a quiet screen, #e9edf3 on #0a0a0d is louder than any accent, which is why titles are white and never blue.
  • The grid is measured in cells because the renderer is. A mock that specifies 12px of padding is a mock that cannot be built in OpenTUI; specifying "one blank row" survives the trip into React and out to the terminal intact.
  • Chrome heights are fixed so screens are interchangeable. Header 2 / footer 2 / statusbar 1 means F6, F7 and F8 swap the body without a single element moving — the eye keeps its anchors across views.
  • Numbers are tabular and right-aligned everywhere. This is the single highest-leverage rule in the system: it turns every list into a column you can scan for the outlier without reading a word.
