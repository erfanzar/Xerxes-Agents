Decisions

  • The header names the base. "working tree vs HEAD" plus the branch and commit sha, because +128 −42 is meaningless until you know what it is measured against. Same header slot would read "vs origin/main" or "vs stash@{0}" — never just "DIFF".
  • The file index only exists when there is room for it. Below ~110 columns it collapses into the header's "1 of 6" counter and ⇥; the diff body never gives up width, since the body is the reason you opened the view.
  • Paths truncate from the left (…/opentui/agentPanel.tsx), because the filename is the identifying end of a path. The index shows tails; the pane header above the diff always shows the selected file's full path, so the elision never costs you the answer.
  • Word-level highlights are the point, row tints are the frame. The row background says "this line changed"; the brighter span says "these four tokens changed". Without the second layer, a re-indented line looks identical to a rewritten one.
  • Cyan is reserved for structure — hunk headers here, and nothing else on this screen. It is not a fourth state colour; green and red keep their exact meanings from every other view (added/succeeded, removed/failed).
  • Two number gutters, not one. Old and new line numbers side by side is what makes a unified diff navigable when you are typing a line number into an editor in the other window.
