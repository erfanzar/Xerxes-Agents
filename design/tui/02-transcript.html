Decisions

  • Three voices, three shapes. You (full-width tinted band with a blue rail), the model (plain indented prose, no box, no colour), the machine (⏺ rows with a leader and a number). You never have to read a label to know who is speaking — the geometry already told you.
  • Thinking collapses to one line, permanently. Reasoning is evidence, not narrative: it gets a ▸, a leader, and a right-aligned token count so you can price it at a glance and expand it with ⇥ only when a turn goes wrong.
  • Every tool row ends in a number. Duration right-aligned on a dotted leader turns a wall of calls into a scannable column — the 4.1s failure is findable without reading a single tool name.
  • The failed Bash row stays in the transcript and stays red. Retries are the story of the turn; hiding the first attempt would make the 5-tool ledger a lie.
  • The turn ledger └ 5 tools · 6.2k tok · 38s closes each answer with its cost. It is the only summary; there is no separate stats pane to go look at.
  • The live row uses the same blue ● as a working agent card on F6. One dot vocabulary across screens is worth more than any per-screen cleverness.
